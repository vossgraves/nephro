# Nephro light-theme cinematic background video brief

## Purpose

Create a silent, loop-safe hero background video for a renal-function calculator landing page with an entirely **light, clinical visual system**. The visual must communicate a path from biological signal to transparent calculation without making the page feel dark, dramatic, or decorative-only.

## Shared art direction

Use a bright foundation throughout: warm white, pearl, very pale gray, soft silver, and restrained charcoal linework. The video should appear to live inside a premium clinical editorial environment rather than against a black void. Add subtle glass, paper, or frosted-panel textures only where they improve depth. There must be no teal, blue, green, red, black-background field, or saturated UI accent. The result should be calm, accurate, contemporary, and non-gory.

## Narrative beats

1. Open on a floating, stylized but anatomically recognizable kidney against a nearly white, softly graduated background. Fine silver particles and delicate filtration-like lines suggest renal function.
2. Pull back to reveal a generic clinician in a light neutral coat or charcoal scrubs, working with a thin tablet on a pale clinical desk. The clinician’s face is not a focal point; hands and tablet interaction matter most.
3. The clinician taps and swipes the tablet. The camera tracks to the screen, translating the organic kidney signal into an orderly calculator context.
4. Push into the tablet screen until it becomes a bright white, high-contrast abstract interface plane with charcoal blocks, rules, and numeric-like geometry but **no readable text**. The final frame must leave clean, bright negative space for the HTML `Open calculator` CTA.
5. Keep the motion slow and legible within an 8–12 second loop, with a held final tablet state that supports the CTA.

## Desktop composition

Use a wide composition with a softly off-white, low-detail text-safe region in the left 36%. Stage the kidney and clinician/tablet toward the right two-thirds. The final tablet frame should be centered-right and bright, allowing charcoal headline text and a dark primary CTA to remain clear on the left.

## Mobile composition

Use a separate portrait composition, not a crop of the desktop master. Reserve a pale, low-detail text-safe band across the upper quarter; stage the kidney in the upper-middle and the clinician’s hands/tablet in the lower-middle. The tablet final state should be readable in portrait, but the action must always remain in semantic HTML outside the video.

## Site-wide theme notes

The page will use a white/very-light-gray background, charcoal typography, cool-gray borders, a near-black primary button, and no dark hero section. The video will receive a soft white gradient veil so it is present but never competes with text. A static poster will appear for reduced motion, data-saver behavior, or unsupported autoplay.
